## Play the new slice

1. Start a new night. You have two bandages and no gun. Existing campaigns imported through Continue retain their equipment.
2. Follow the amber marker to **Sella in Market Court**, beside the clinic. Press E. Her shipping-paper favor needs no money or Mara job.
3. Carry the papers to Neri inside the clinic, press E, then return the signed receipt to Sella. Her stock supplies one pistol and six rounds. If you acquired a pistol along the way, the favor pays six rounds instead.
4. Equip with 2 and press R to load. **1 puts guns away**. A patrol that sees a drawn gun can pursue and confiscate it; unseen inventory does not reveal possession. Shooting and attacks retain the existing shared police response.
5. Earn money with Mara or cargo and return to Sella. The shotgun and SMG are separate purchases; ammunition costs extra and comes from finite stock. Compare short bursts with a full SMG magazine. The prepared weapon test level supplies all three guns for practice.
6. Look for **Tomas**, carrying the supply pack between the freight post, Sella and Neri. Three consignments contain 12 pistol rounds, two shells, 24 SMG rounds and two medical doses each. Blocking his path delays the actual goods. E inspects his remaining cargo; taking it leaves the destinations short. Stolen doses stay in your bag until given to Neri or sold to Mara.

Sella's pistol can also be stolen, and downed armed people can lose their gun and remaining ammunition to E recovery. Repeated looting does not duplicate stock. A witnessed theft closes Sella's trade. After a confiscation, unfinished favors, cargo and Mara's work offer ways back; the stock and three consignments remain finite.

## Controls and saves

WASD moves, Shift sprints, Ctrl sneaks, mouse aims, left mouse fires. 1 conceals/fists, 2 pistol, 3 shotgun, 4 SMG, R reload, B bandage, E interact, Space tactical pause, Tab map, F5 save. F at home opens rest and supplies. The main-menu **DEBUG FAST RUN** button enables triple sprint speed and unlimited stamina for this application session; it starts OFF and is never saved in the campaign.

Demo 05 uses `arms-progress.json` in `%USERPROFILE%/AppData/LocalLow/Funstra/Funstra`. Continue can import `streets-progress.json` or earlier saves without overwriting them. Automated routes use separate test saves and remain muted.

## Scope

One dealer, one walking freight courier, three bounded consignments, three firearms. Delivery pauses when its recipient is incapacitated; stabilize them to resume. There is no infinite ammunition restock. Rifle squads, army trucks, grenades and 30-plus soldiers remain later slices. Existing police pressure remains nine pistol officers delivered by two response trucks.

Guided validation distinguishes controller travel from staged transactions and accelerated delivery simulation. It does not establish human input feel or performance on lower-end hardware. See [validation](Evidence/Demo05/validation-summary.json) for the final build identity and results.
