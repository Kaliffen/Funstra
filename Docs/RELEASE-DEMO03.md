# Keep the Lights On — Demo 03 (0.3.0)

Earn Neri's partnership, repair a shared refuge, and decide who gets the clinic's last medicine. Recover without new debt, contribute to the clinic fund, and buy from its finite supplier reserve. Your power now includes a place and people who depend on your decisions.

Neri and Ivo respond to how you obtained medicine, treated people and handled their claims. Old Port now has moving cars that yield, solid bins and crates, improved companion routes, readable appointments and history, and a pettable Tally whose familiarity survives saving.

Four persona reviewers assessed guided runs of the Demo 03 candidate and each awarded 8/10 for different reasons. Their original reviews remain intact in the [single Demo 03 dossier](https://kaliffen.github.io/Funstra/dossier.html). This publication pass closes the reserve-policy boundary test gap and adds actual campaign captures showing Mara's RUNNER and CONNECTED standing. It does not change the reviewed gameplay rules.

## Start playing

Extract the entire portable archive and run Funstra.exe. Start at cyan REPAIR. F opens the clinic refuge; P pets Tally; Space pauses for tactics. The included KEEP-THE-LIGHTS-ON.md gives the review route. Earlier demo saves are imported without changing their original files.

## Limits

This is a free prototype, not a completed sandbox campaign. Medicine is a finite local economy; patients are scheduled transactions. The refuge is represented outside and in its management screen. Cars are ambient and can wait behind pedestrians.

Only our RTX 4090 machine is available for testing. Low-end compatibility remains a target, not a verified minimum-spec claim. Guided gameplay, source inspection and screenshots do not substitute for human difficulty or input-feel assessment. Human acceptance remains open.
