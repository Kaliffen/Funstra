Police Response makes witnessed violence bring armed patrols and physical truck reinforcements to Old Port. It carries forward the expanded district, enterable clinic, simulated pistol/shotgun combat, recorded sound effects and four prepared foundation levels developed since Demo03.

- Police share the gunman's reported last position; unidentified gunfire prompts an area search.
- Officers fire traveling projectiles, spend finite ammunition and reload. Two trucks deliver six reinforcements, for nine police maximum.
- Dispatch progress, casualties and magazines survive reload. Pause stops the response; defeat leaves a recoverable campaign.
- Fixed empty-ammunition patrol stalls, stale dispatch requests, patrol recovery positions and a traffic-yield regression.

Validation: 2,201 editor checks and 268 core exported-player checks passed. Four independent guided reviewer runs each passed 35 focused checks and scored the increment 7/10. The ZIP was extracted and its executable passed the focused route again. All automated gameplay tests were muted.

Limits: truck logistics and a short live patrol exchange are verified separately; sustained nine-officer combat pressure remains unproven. Tactical-pause overlap and generic recovery wording have follow-up tickets. Trucks remain parked after search, with finite personnel until recovery resets the response. Rifle squads and the 32-soldier army/rifle/grenade tier are planned, not included. Testing used an RTX4090; no minimum-spec or human input-feel claim.
