# Night work

## The neighborhood
Town footprint: 100 x 100 m. North is the depot and waterfront. South is the night market, player safehouse and Mara's pawn counter. Two north/south streets and three east/west streets form six blocks. Alleys offer shorter routes and break police sight. Solid perimeter prevents leaving the playable area.

Readable colors: mint = player / safe contact; amber = objective / valuable; red = danger; blue = police; muted coral, teal and indigo = town. Warm windows and shop signs give dusk contrast. Camera stays high enough to read routes; buildings cut away when they obscure the player.

## 01 — Borrowed time
Mara: "Welcome to Funstra. Someone left my delivery by the arcade. Bring it here. Keep the blue coats out of it."
Target: courier bag behind the arcade, $120. Take time: 1.4 seconds.
One nearby patrol. Teaches moving, interacting and returning to the contact. Completion grants reputation and one perk.

## 02 — The back door
Mara: "Vico keeps our money in a box behind his garage. Consider this a withdrawal. If anyone sees you, use the alleys."
Target: garage lockbox, $220. Take time: 4 seconds.
Two overlapping patrols. Teaches planning timing, sneaking, escaping and hiding. Completion grants reputation and another perk.

## 03 — Paper trail
Mara: "One last favor. The depot's ledger has names that shouldn't be there. Bring it back clean and your debt is gone."
Target: depot ledger, $400. Take time: 5 seconds.
The depot alarm always raises heat. Player must break sight and escape before delivery. Final delivery completes the demo.

## RPG choices
- Light feet: sneak speed +35%, slightly shorter detection range.
- Long stride: sprint speed +20%, stamina capacity +35%.
- Quick hands: theft interaction time -40%.
Choose one previously unchosen perk after each of the first two jobs.

## Failure and continuation
Police must remain within arrest range for a short duration; escaping interrupts arrest. Arrest returns the player to the safehouse, removes only the currently carried item and up to $40, clears pursuit, resets the current target. Completed jobs and perks remain. No lives or unrecoverable fail states.

Progress checkpointed at job delivery, perk selection, and arrest. Reload starts at the safehouse with unfinished theft reset. No save during active chase.
