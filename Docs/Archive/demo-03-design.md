# Funstra development direction

The [vision](VISION.md) and [world origin](WORLD.md) remain the foundation. The power arc is vulnerable individual → partnership → crew → neighborhood foothold → institution. Each step changes what the player can rely on and what they become responsible for.

## Current review candidate

**Demo 03: Keep the Lights On, version 0.3.0.** [Playable scope and review route](KEEP-THE-LIGHTS-ON.md). [Validation](Evidence/VALIDATION.md).

This slice deepens the same people and place. An earned partnership becomes a shared refuge through a $120 investment, split between repairs and a clinic fund. The refuge supports recovery without new debt. Players can fund the clinic, draw on the finite supplier reserve and choose between public care and protecting the last two doses for the crew. Public treatment and dialogue reflect that choice. This is the first small foothold, not a completed organization-management game.

Neri and Ivo respond in prose to existing consequences. Optional conversations connect their families to the debt system. The arrival appointment is visible during play, with the countdown available in the journal or collector screen. Mara's rank is explicitly scoped to Mara. Scheduled incidents show their event time.

Street quality includes four moving cars, junction access, pedestrian yielding, dynamic route obstacles, solid environmental and collectible props, safe placement when importing older positions, and an interaction with Tally.

## Review interpretation

The supplied dossier reports harness runs, screen inspection and source reading. Its strongest criticisms concern the gap between real state and what the player understands or feels. We accepted those findings without treating the scores as acceptance of this build or adding a disconnected content catalogue.

The differing opinions on countdowns were resolved through presentation: show a fixed appointment beside the existing clock, and leave a numerical countdown in detailed inspection screens. The lower-tier hardware gap remains because a capped run on this machine cannot close it.

## Deliberate limits

Medicine and relevant funds form a finite local economy. Public treatment is a scheduled transaction; it does not require a physically visiting patient actor. Edda is characterized in dialogue, not implemented as a separate agent. The refuge has an exterior visual change and management screen, not an enterable room. Treatment, rest and repairs use immediate prototype transactions.

Neri remains a medical support companion with autonomous movement and care. Direct squad control and companion attacks are deferred. Combat retains fists, a pistol, bleeding, incapacitation and recovery. Detailed anatomy, permanent deaths, custody, renewable supplies, local production and faction plans are future work.

Traffic is ambient local driving. Cars do not deal damage, accept a driver or dispatch reinforcements. Congested lanes may wait for pedestrians. Original cargo and shop replenishment remain prototype rules outside the conserved medical ledger.

## Acceptance

The candidate must pass editor rules, exported-player regressions, new refuge/traffic/collision checks and rendered inspection before delivery. Final results and limitations live in Evidence/VALIDATION.md. User acceptance of Demo 03 remains pending.

## Next decision

Evaluate whether caring for a place changes the player's choices. If it does, connect a named person's actual work, needs and movement to a renewable supply route, with opportunities to protect, disrupt or appropriate it. That would address the finite economy's ceiling while strengthening the sandbox foundations. Do not add another resource bar unless its decisions and consequences are visible in the street.
