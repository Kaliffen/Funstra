# Nell's notes from the table-read

Okay so, before I write the actual review I want to get down what happened at the
group chat while it's fresh, because I always forget the funny bits by the time I
sit down to type.

It was me, Dag, Priya and Marcus, all four of us in the same call, which honestly
never not feels a bit like being seated at the wrong dinner table — Dag wants to
talk about whether the dose economy closes correctly and I want to talk about
whether there's a cat. But that's the point of us, I think, so fine.

Going in, we were looking at the earlier Hot Cargo build, and I said my bar for it
was genuinely just: would I want to come back to this tomorrow night. Not "is it
deep," not "does it mean something" — just, is forty minutes with this a nice
forty minutes. I said I wanted rhythm, not depth. And I flagged straight away that
I bounce off timers and any real threat of loss, so if the heat/alarm/wanted stuff
in that build read as punishing rather than tense, I was going to feel it more
than the others would, and they should discount my grumbling about that
accordingly. That's just a known thing about me at this point.

I also, and I will die on this hill, said I had not yet gotten confirmation of a
cat. This turned out to be a very reasonable thing to ask about, thank you.

Since then the organizer has run three other people through the actual newer
build — Demo 02, "A Bed & a Bandage" — ahead of me:

- **Marcus** did the older Hot Cargo build technically, 7/10, performance and bugs.
- **Dag** did Demo 02 for systems — does the medicine economy, the clock, the
  combat rules actually hold together as a simulation. He gave it 7/10 and by
  all accounts had a lovely time watching numbers behave correctly.
- **Priya** did Demo 02 for meaning — does any of it land emotionally, does the
  pay/steal/fight choice actually weigh anything. She gave it 4/10, mixed on
  Neri, felt the big choice had no narrative teeth.

I'm the fourth and last one through the pipeline for this build, and nobody
before me was asking my question, which is just: is this a nice hour? Not "is
the machine correct" (Dag), not "does it mean something" (Priya) — just, do I
come away from a session feeling good, or do I come away feeling like I did
homework. That's my whole job here.

Going in to actually play it I expected the shipment clock and the combat to be
where I'd bounce off, same worry as before. We'll see below whether that held up
or whether the game was kinder to me than I gave it credit for.
