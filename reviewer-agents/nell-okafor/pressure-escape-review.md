# Pressure and Escape 0.4.2 — Nell Okafor

**8/10. Guided scripted build review, 8 September 2026.** The frightening bit now has a readable ending. That makes a considerable difference to whether I would willingly start it.

Last time I asked the game to tell me when hiding was working. This time the inspected map screen says **SEARCH / 9 OFFICERS**, and the eventual message says, “Immediate pursuit has ended. Recorded incidents remain.” You can understand both halves: you may breathe again, and the evening still happened. I like that much more than watching an armed-response label and wondering whether somebody knows precisely which wall I am behind.

The guided escape also gets somewhere. The ordinary-health player withdrew around buildings, saved and reloaded during pursuit, waited out the search, and travelled into the clinic. The final frame shows the opened clinic interior and Neri's marker beside the player. Last review's homeward journey was a request; now there is a demonstrated route back to a recognisable place. That is a meaningful improvement even though this pass does not continue into a conversation, treatment or a quiet evening routine.

Pause is easier to trust and easier to look through. My ammunition stays above the instructions, the notification has its own strip, and an unrecruited companion no longer comes with a useless orders grid. Resume is obvious. The runtime checks also confirm that the prepared nine-officer encounter stops dealing damage or advancing its search while paused. I can stop to think without losing the fight while reading about it.

You do still need to leave promptly. The prepared exposed player actually reached defeat through live combat; the separate early withdrawal arrived safely with 100 health. These are useful opposite outcomes, not a human difficulty curve. The escape harness already knew the route and left almost immediately. I cannot promise that somebody who panics, takes a wrong turn and then remembers the bandage will find the same breathing room. This particular escape never needed a bandage.

Recovery is especially considerate about uncertainty. The fresh street defeat says you lost no cash or carried goods and keeps equipment and completed work. The loaded-loss fixture gives a readable list: $17 cash, $60 cargo, Mara's item, medicine and Ivo's existing recognition, with directions for recovering the recoverable things. The $40 emergency-care debt and 45 health are still clear. You do not need to wonder whether the game quietly took something else, or invented a medicine job you had never touched.

There are small remaining irritations. On the expanded map, Ivo's label covers part of the nearby garage label. And the emotional recovery is still ahead of the physical return: I see the clinic and can infer relief, but this route does not show somebody receiving me after the pursuit. I would replay for that ordinary next step, and for a wounded, less perfectly timed escape. Neither is evidence that the implemented return is broken.

**Would I put this on after a bad day?** I would be more comfortable doing so now that pause, hiding and losses explain themselves. Deliberately starting the nine-officer response would still be a choice for an evening when I want tension. This 8 is for the bounded increment's clearer boundaries and demonstrated way home, up from my Police Response 7. It does not claim a month of enjoyment or a fresh review of the cat and refuge systems.
