# Nell Okafor — Nobody Gets Home Alone v0.6.0

**Game now 5.3/10 · Slice delivery 8.3–9.6/10 provisional · Evidence 89%**  
8 September 2026. Independent **guided scripted build review**, evaluation protocol v2. The largest missing experience is a satisfying life with these people after the finite errands and rescue have ended.

I can now bring someone home, and the game knows who did it. That is the part I would tell a friend about. In my prepared casualty scene, Neri took control while the protagonist stayed down, carried them around the clinic wall and through the doorway, and spent an existing clinic dose. The screen then said, “NERI carried me to the clinic. I know who came back.” You can see the people in the room afterward. That is considerably more convincing than a recovery counter beside an inaccessible building. [Carry and admission](../../Evidence/Demo06/review-nell/C05b-physical-carry.png), [clinic aftermath](../../Evidence/Demo06/review-nell/C06b-clinic-admission.png).

Rell also gives you a reasonable way to begin. You can do twelve seconds of actual repair work with his last gasket and earn his company before attempting the yard. His impounded component remains there; helping him has not conjured a second prize or quietly completed the dangerous job. The separate paid outing states its $90 terms clearly and lets you come back without a fight. I like being able to prepare through useful work. [Offer](../../Evidence/Demo06/review-nell/C08-rell-workshop-offer.png), [repair result](../../Evidence/Demo06/review-nell/C09-repaired-workshop-partnership.png), [Vale's terms](../../Evidence/Demo06/review-nell/C01-vale-release-terms.png).

The world is allowed to hurt. I am not asking it to guarantee everybody's return. Leaving Rell behind kept his body and an explicit memory through reload; it did not quietly replace him with a healthy companion. The nine-officer route has both actual defeat and an escape. My smaller rifle encounter reached cover wounded, then another real hit interrupted treatment before it finished. Keeping the dressing in that situation is fair; offering a false promise of safety would not be. These are incapacitation and abandonment mechanics, with existing emergency recovery after total defeat, not irreversible death. [Abandonment](../../Evidence/Demo06/review-nell/C07-remembered-abandonment.png), [own runtime result](../../Evidence/Demo06/review-nell/crew-runtime-result.txt).

What still deflates me is how quickly the people become instructions and inventory again. The rescue sentence is welcome, but I did not see it develop into a changed conversation, an ordinary visit or a later request between the crew. Rell's finished objective becomes “Keep your crew alive.” That is a responsibility, not much of a reason to start another evening. There are existing cargo runs, clinic services, a refuge and Tally; I am giving them credit. They still amount to a limited routine around a small number of finite situations. A larger cast list or another gun does not repair that particular absence.

The resident bridge takes a useful step. Elias visibly comes over to thank you, and his panel explains why. The shared resident route distinguishes somebody who returns to help from somebody who chooses safety, with a real finite dressing spent. However, that conduct remains easier to learn from an explanatory panel than from these small, similar figures. The clinic's warm doorway works; much of the surrounding port still looks like repeated pale blocks with dark streets between them. I do not yet get much of the salt-worn working society described in WORLD from the places alone. [Recognition](../../Evidence/Demo06/residents-final/R05-recognition.png), [resident method](../../Evidence/Demo06/residents-final/method.txt).

**Would I put this on after a bad day?** For a bounded repair or a careful paid outing, perhaps. For an evening of attachment and satisfying routine, not yet. Pause and explicit losses help me trust the rules; they do not supply the missing evening. This is not a request to remove casualties or make the yard easier.

## Largest shortcomings and actionable findings

1. **The return does not yet sustain another evening.** Breadth loses 1.2 weighted points. Finite errands and resource trips provide activity, but the completed pump does not yield a varied ongoing life. Replay a saved completed operation into a consequential next outing and an ordinary return visit; it need not add another district or remove danger.
2. **Understanding the crew still takes too much menu work.** Control/clarity loses 1.2. C05–C07 show the same full action grid for healthy, carried and abandoned targets. Old trade/clinic/cargo interactions require the protagonist ([manual](../../NOBODY-GETS-HOME-ALONE.md), [interaction branch](../../Assets/Scripts/FunstraGame.cs)); a player continuing as Neri must understand that limit. Show unavailable-action reasons and make the ordinary-service boundary visible at the point of interaction. A companion-led visit after protagonist downing would resolve the practical impact. This is a documented capability/clarity limitation, not an observed softlock.
3. **Remembered people are not yet developed relationships.** People loses 1.0. The rescue and abandonment facts matter, but subsequent crew conversation/behavior barely develops their meaning. Compare a later ordinary encounter after rescue versus abandonment, keeping the same people and resources. The deficit concerns response and attachment, not a requirement to forgive or resurrect anyone.

Other findings:

- **Confirmed minor presentation defect:** own C00 title reads Demo06 at the top and **“DEMO 04 / FOUNDATION CANDIDATE”** at the bottom. Correct the release label; verify a fresh title capture. Craft credit already includes this issue.
- **Confirmed UI/readability weakness:** C01a and C22 crowd actor status/world labels around the quay/guard group; useful words compete with one another. Prioritize nearby actionable labels. The shared E04 map separates the former Ivo/garage labels better than my prior review; the new Rell marker still shares dense map territory.
- **Source-supported learning limitation:** `medicinePractice` and `mechanicsPractice` are incremented/stored/validated, but the inspected references do not change skills or expose medical practice in the crew panel ([Crew.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/FunstraCrew.cs), [CrewUI.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/FunstraCrewUI.cs), [DockOperation.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/FunstraDockOperation.cs), [CrewState.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/CrewState.cs)). Give completed medical work readable progression feedback. Any later mechanical skill effect is a whole-game opportunity, not an invented frozen-ledger promise.
- **Presentation preference grounded in frames:** the resident status panel can dominate the clinic aftermath with an unrelated nearby resident's routine (own C06b). Consider giving the arrival/rescue the foreground briefly. This does not invalidate the successful clinic transaction.
- **Coverage gaps:** complete crew-enabled livelihood/session restart, learning feedback beyond inspected states, long-run economy and unfamiliar-player escape. No identical rerun is justified merely to inflate confidence in human difficulty.
