# Nell's notes from the second table-read

Okay, so, quick catch-up before I write the actual review, same as last time, because I
will absolutely forget the funny parts otherwise.

Same four of us, same call, same slightly-wrong-dinner-table energy — Dag opened with a
sentence about a comparison operator before anyone had said hello, which, genuinely, I
love that about him even while I have no idea what he's talking about half the time.

Quick recap of where everyone landed on Demo 02, "A Bed & a Bandage," because it matters
for how I'm scoring this one:

- Me: 7/10. Warm town, lovely HUD, a surprisingly gentle defeat screen ("STILL
  BREATHING" as a hug, not a slap), a caretaking loop with Neri that I actually wanted to
  keep doing. My one real complaint, the thing I said I'd die on the hill about: there is
  a cat, Tally, confirmed to exist by the design doc, and I could not pet him, name him,
  or do anything with him except believe he was asleep somewhere off-screen. I said, in
  so many words, "next demo, let me feed the cat. That's genuinely my top note."
- Dag: 7/10, systems held together, dose economy closed.
- Priya: 4/10, the big choice had no narrative teeth, Neri read more like a mechanic
  than a person.
- Marcus: 7/10 on the older Hot Cargo build, technical/performance pass.

Now we've all been through Demo 03, "Keep the Lights On," and everyone landed on 8. Dag,
8, because the ledger absorbed a new spend path without cracking and the reserve
threshold is legible on the same screen that causes it. Marcus, 8, because a three-way
regression check across all three demo generations came back clean and a stat he almost
mis-flagged turned out to be a genuine, better-explained fix rather than a recurrence of
his old REPUTATION bug. Priya, 8, and this is the one I actually read twice, because the
exact hole she dug into last time — three routes with Ivo all converging on "everything
is accounted for" — is now five distinct, specific, written-by-a-person dialogue states,
and Neri's side has eleven. She quoted a line about towels and inherited debt that made
me stop scrolling.

So going in to actually play this myself, I had one specific, very small, very
personal thing I was checking for, and it wasn't the ledger or the reserve threshold or
whether Ivo sounds like a person now (though obviously I read Priya's lines and they're
lovely). It was: did they let me pet the cat. That's it. That's the whole bar I walked
in with. Everything else about this build is gravy as far as my own review axis goes —
I'm not the systems reviewer and I'm not the meaning reviewer, I'm the "is this a nice
hour, and can I check in on a small animal" reviewer, and I already know that about
myself.
