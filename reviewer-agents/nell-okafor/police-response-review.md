# Police Response 0.4.1 — Nell Okafor

**7/10. Guided scripted build review, 8 September 2026.** A more convincing dangerous evening, with a reassuring way back out. I would still choose the clinic on a bad day.

I like that the street now notices what you do. The blue trucks arrive on an actual road, and officers stand beside them instead of simply appearing as a number somewhere. In the inspected deployment frame, their red alert marks make the interruption immediately understandable. This is a city answering an attack. It gives the quieter things in town something to exist alongside.

My favourite part is still the permission to stop. The tactical pause clearly says time has stopped, and the route actually checked that both the dispatch clock and live return fire stopped. After the live officer wounded the exposed player, the message said, “Hit! B bandage / SPACE pause / break sight.” That is useful, short advice at exactly the moment I would need it. The eventual recovery screen remains readable and kind: **STILL BREATHING**, 45 health, and an explicit $40 debt. There is a consequence without throwing away the evening's story.

But I want a little more help understanding when I have successfully got away. The upper-right line tells me **ARMED RESPONSE / 3 OFFICERS** during the live encounter. In this version, the status text stays “ARMED RESPONSE” while an identified gunman is being searched for; it does not distinguish a current sighting from a last-known-position search. The rules can remember less than I fear they know, but the interface does not help me feel that relief. A plain “LOST SIGHT / SEARCHING” would do more for my comfort than an exact countdown. This is a clarity request, not evidence that the officers see through walls: the hidden-position fixture passed.

The pause screen also takes a lot of the street away just when I want to look at it. In all three tactical frames the central message overlaps the pause panel's lower area; the large crew panel is present even though I have no companion. I can still find Resume and read the response count. I would like to inspect the trucks and possible escape routes with less furniture in front of them. That matters more as the eventual number of soldiers grows.

My earlier Demo03 score was 8, earned by attachment, the cat, and a warmer home. This 7 is for the narrower police increment, not a claim that those earlier improvements broke or disappeared. I did not revisit the cat or refuge routine in this pass. This increment makes the danger more credible and keeps recovery humane, but it has not yet shown me how an ordinary forty-minute evening settles after a frightening encounter.

**Would I put this on after a bad day?** For a quiet clinic visit, potentially; this route did not retest that evening. For deliberately provoking the police, only on a day when I want tension. The game is allowed to be dangerous. I want the danger's boundaries to be easy to read.
