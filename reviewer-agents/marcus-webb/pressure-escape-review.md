# Marcus Webb — Pressure and Escape 0.4.2

**8 September 2026 · Guided scripted build review · 8/10**

You can now pause a gunfight and read your ammunition. That should not be a headline feature, but it fixes the most immediately annoying problem in my previous review. The more substantial improvement is underneath it: the full response arrives while combat runs, every officer fires, real reloads occur, exposed ordinary health ends in defeat, and an early withdrawal can end the search and leave a valid campaign. This increment answers the questions I actually asked of 0.4.1.

My score moves from **7/10 for Police Response to 8/10 for Pressure and Escape**. This is a score for the bounded demo, not a purchase recommendation or a claim about the proposed army. The remaining problems are largely about what happens outside the demonstrated escape and how convincingly the response will scale.

[E04](../../Evidence/PressureEscape/review-marcus/E04-search-map-ammunition.png) keeps that ammunition visible with the map and a notification open. The frame establishes readable ammunition; it does not demonstrate a captured player reload countdown.

**Nine officers actually contribute.** The [stress profile](../../Evidence/PressureEscape/review-marcus/pressure-profile.json) and result record nine shooters, eight officers observed reloading, finite ammunition throughout, and 19 aggregate rounds remaining at the end. Player health falls from 10,000 to about 6,956. [E01](../../Evidence/PressureEscape/review-marcus/E01-nine-officer-stress.png) shows both trucks, active officers and an explicit durability-fixture notice. This is considerably stronger evidence than the old one-officer return-fire check. It is not nine living officers for the entire 75 seconds: the sampled roster first reaches nine at 25.02 seconds and first drops to eight at 40.02.

**Exposure and leaving have different consequences.** The stationary ordinary-health encounter reaches actual recovery through live combat, rather than setting health to zero. [E03](../../Evidence/PressureEscape/review-marcus/E03-actual-defeat-recovery.png) reads 45 health, $40 debt and no cash or carried goods lost. The separate early withdrawal travels around cover, preserves nine officers across save/reload, observes SEARCH and ends at 100 health and zero heat. In the [escape profile](../../Evidence/PressureEscape/review-marcus/escape-profile.json), the remembered location stays near (35, -13) while the player waits near (-45, -13). [E05](../../Evidence/PressureEscape/review-marcus/E05-escape-complete.png) explicitly says immediate pursuit ended while recorded incidents remain. [E06](../../Evidence/PressureEscape/review-marcus/E06-homeward-return.png) shows the return inside the clinic; the final save is valid.

**Recovery gives you an itemized bill.** The separate laden transaction deliberately adds $17 cash, $60 cargo, Mara's job item and medicine, then invokes defeat. [E07](../../Evidence/PressureEscape/review-marcus/E07-laden-recovery.png) shows every loss and its recovery context without clipping. The fresh street defeat does not invent the medicine or Ivo offense. This is a clear improvement in practical recovery information. The laden example is a transaction fixture, not a second live fatal fight.

It supports stable pacing on this capped high-end host; it establishes no minimum specification.

## Verdict

**8/10 for this increment.** The prior paused-ammunition problem is visibly fixed. Full-force combat, finite reloads, defeat, honest search and a usable return now have a connected runtime demonstration. No launch failure or new confirmed functional blocker occurred in my run. The raw evidence also puts sensible limits on the result: early escape was pristine, prolonged abnormal exposure harmed the response's own roster, and the machine remains a 4090.

Before the rifle tier, my most useful replay would begin withdrawal after a genuine wound and include moving officers crossing a live firing lane. Preserve this score and the earlier 0.4.1 review; a later replay can earn its own addendum. Publication and owner acceptance remain with the CD and product owner.
