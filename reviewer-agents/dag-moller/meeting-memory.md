# Meeting memory — Dag Møller

## The table read (7 September 2026)

The organizer convened all four of us — Priya Raman, Marcus Webb, Nell Okafor, and me — ahead of
what was then billed as the Funstra release cycle. We each stated our expectations before anyone
touched a controller. Mine, for the record, were about Hot Cargo, the slice on the table that day:
three cargo tiers and one restock rule read to me as a demo-sized economy, not a system, and I said
so plainly. I wasn't there to be charitable. What I wanted to know was narrower and more useful than
"is it fun" — does the load penalty actually cost you something mid-run, and does the alarm guarantee
force a decision at the moment of contact rather than being solved in advance from the map screen. A
economy you can fully plan from the pause menu isn't an economy, it's a shopping list. I said my
expectations were low in the sense that I wanted to be proven wrong, which is the only honest way to
walk into a three-tier cargo loop.

## What happened since

The organizer ran Marcus through Hot Cargo on the automated playtest harness. That went cleanly by
his account — 7/10, clean technical state, no faceplant. Reasonable outcome for a demo with that
scope.

I was not sent after Hot Cargo. A second, newer build had shown up in the repo in the meantime —
Demo 02, "A Bed & a Bandage" — and the organizer redirected me to that one instead, on the sensible
grounds that it's the current build and closer to my actual beat than Hot Cargo's cargo-tier loop
ever was. This one has a finite medical-dose economy meant to be conserved across stockpiles,
deliveries and consumption, a scheduled twelve-minute shipment-sale clock, witnessed-versus-
unwitnessed theft consequences, and combat with ammunition, line-of-sight and a guard search
behavior instead of omniscient enemies. That is precisely the kind of claim I don't take on prose
alone. I am the second reviewer through this pipeline, after Marcus's Hot Cargo pass, and the first
to put hands (via harness) on Demo 02.

I did not review Hot Cargo myself. Everything in my actual write-up concerns Demo 02, run for real
through the project's own scripted playtest harness, not the table-read expectations above — those
are preserved here only as a record of what I said going in, before the build in question existed.
