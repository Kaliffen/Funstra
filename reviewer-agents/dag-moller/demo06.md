## What the expedition establishes

Paying Vale makes sense because the alternative has an actual cost structure. My solo route spends $90, collects the one impeller and returns it to Rell; payment is not a teleport or a second reward object. The shared final approach route separately takes that same component unseen with the protagonist and with Neri, using the north loading edge behind the watch. The held protagonist does not move when Neri takes control. These are credible different uses of the same place, though the two theft examples do not establish much need for a partner: Neri performs the collector's journey while the protagonist waits.

The final shared assault uses $800 prepared purchasing money, Neri's existing pistol, a bought SMG and a physically transferred rifle. All three fire their own finite ammunition at ordinary-health opponents. The actual outcome was a returned component, the protagonist at **41.54 health**, and Neri and Rell at **100**. The useful cost is not just the wound: the protagonist spent **10 SMG rounds**, Neri **7 pistol rounds**, and Rell **5 rifle rounds**. The [raw combat receipt](../../Evidence/Demo06/approach-final/crew-combat-outcome.json) retains 38 SMG rounds, 11 pistol rounds and 15 rifle rounds. Its per-actor line shows zero for the holstered protagonist, so using that line alone would falsely report exhaustion. The full arrays preserve the actual weapons and ammunition across reload. The receipt also openly allows only the existing uncarried protagonist's small upward Y clearance on resume; it does not silently normalize wounds, custody or horizontal position.

Rescue is the most valuable addition. A downed protagonist leaves a playable Neri at her own location. A body has to go around the same wall as its carrier. Returning it spends an existing clinic dose, restores the protagonist to 45 health and records who came back. Leaving Rell behind retains his zero health and location through reload. The [carry](../../Evidence/Demo06/review-dag/C05b-physical-carry.png), [admission](../../Evidence/Demo06/review-dag/C06b-clinic-admission.png) and [abandonment panel](../../Evidence/Demo06/review-dag/C07-remembered-abandonment.png) make those states inspectable. I credit the physical dependency, not an invented experience of emotional attachment. The wounds and abandonment setup are staged; this was not an improvised rescue during the assault.

The hostile side has corresponding limits. Losing Vale cancels a pending report; a guard spends one dressing and restores only 25 health, after which the leader withdraws without restoring the lost network. The separate shared pressure run delivers the existing six rifle reinforcements, records all nine officers firing and reloading, and ends the 75-second stress with no ammunition remaining. Five distinct reinforcement owners have actual player-hit receipts. That section gives the protagonist 10,000 health and disables arrest. It proves sustained finite activity, not an ordinary person's survival. Separate ordinary-health exposure loses and controller escape reaches safety. My own delayed rifle withdrawal was wounded; its subsequent treatment was interrupted by another actual rifle hit after about 2.73 simulation seconds, retaining both dressings. Completed self-aid is demonstrated elsewhere in the same run. A PASS here means the interrupted action conserved its resources, not that the treatment succeeded.

The game also contains more than this yard. Same-build shared evidence revisits the dealer favor, confiscation, three physical courier consignments, medicine diversion and shortage, clinic/refuge rules, Mara's three jobs, cargo weight/extraction, police and traffic. These connect money, treatment and exposure usefully. But the new partnership still arrives principally as another controllable body. Rell's pump ends in a changed workshop and a Follow order; the next objective becomes keeping the crew alive. I do not yet have a substantial second use for this particular partnership, or a changing livelihood that repeatedly calls on its choices.

## Verdict and continuity

The old question was whether finite response and a wounded withdrawal would survive contact with the actual game. Current evidence answers substantially more of it: owner/victim receipts, depleted rifle withdrawal, real streets and a treatment that can honestly fail under renewed fire. The new crew also carries its people physically and preserves independent equipment. These are real additions.

They make a competent local outing, not yet a substantially realized organizational sandbox. **5.4 for the game now** makes that distinction explicit. **8.5–9.8 provisional for this slice** acknowledges strong mechanical delivery while keeping incomplete integration and the practice shortfall in the arithmetic. The prior 8/10 Pressure review remains its historical bounded-increment judgment; there is no valid numeric G delta. Added outcomes are a third person, direct control, equipment custody, dock alternatives and physical rescue. The most useful replay is a second consequential outing and a continuous ordinary economy-to-crew session, plus focused fixes for the visible text defects. No game changes, rebuild, package or publication were performed by this reviewer.
