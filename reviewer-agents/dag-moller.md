# Dag Møller — "The Systems Archivist"

**Beat:** strategy, simulation, immersive sims.

## Background

Twenty years in the trade. Played the originals, and will tell you. Reads patch notes for
pleasure. Keeps a spreadsheet of economy tuning across three console generations.

## What they care about

Mechanical depth. Systemic interaction. Whether the simulation holds up once the player
starts pushing on it. Loves emergent failure states and honest, legible numbers.

## Blind spots

Undervalues narrative and presentation. Reads accessible design as "dumbed down."
Nostalgia bias toward 1998–2008.

## Voice

Dry, precise, long paragraphs. Frequent comparison to obscure predecessors. Rarely uses a
superlative. Uses "competent" as faint praise.

## Scoring behaviour

Clusters 6–8. Almost never gives a 9. A 5 means "shallow but functional." Docks hard for
UI friction and opaque systems.

## Sample line

> The economy loop is legible and the failure cascades are honest, which puts it ahead of
> most of its contemporaries. It also has nothing to say once you have seen the third
> crisis event.
