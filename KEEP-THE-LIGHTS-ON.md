# Demo 03 — Keep the Lights On

Run `Build/KeepTheLightsOn/Funstra.exe` or `Play.cmd`. Portable release: `Releases/Funstra-demo-03-windows.zip`; extract the whole archive before opening the executable. Version 0.3.0.

## The next step in power

You earned someone willing to come home with you. Now make a place worth coming home to.

At REPAIR, Neri can tell you why the spare bed matters. Deliver the medicine and recruit them, then put $120 into repairing a shared refuge. Half pays for repairs; half stays in the clinic fund. The change appears on the street as a repaired door and porch light. This is an exterior representation of the room, not an enterable interior.

The refuge gives practical security: recover to 80 health without medicine or new debt. Stop bleeding first. Full recovery still needs medical treatment. Neri benefits from the bed when physically at the clinic; a distant companion is not healed by your visit. A betrayed partnership loses refuge privileges.

The clinic fund is separate from your wallet. Contribute $30 or authorize a $30 purchase of two doses from the supplier's remaining reserve. No transaction creates medicine. There are still twelve doses in the entire medical economy, including consumed stock; this build does not pretend that the port has a complete renewable supply chain.

Choose public care, which can spend the last dose on a stranger, or reserve the last two doses for the crew. Reserved stock causes public patients to be turned away. Neri responds to that consequence. Money, need and your own safety now meet at the same shelf.

## Review route — about 20–30 minutes, depending on approach

1. Continue a Demo 02 save or start a new night. Previous saves and builds remain intact.
2. Watch the street cars from a pavement. Cross in front of one, clear its path, then watch it resume. Pause with Space; traffic stops too.
3. Try walking into a green bin, a cargo crate, the clinic counter and the camp bed. Use cover beside bins, rather than standing inside them.
4. Visit Neri at cyan REPAIR. Read the conversation and ask about the bed. **P** beside Tally pets him; **F** at the clinic opens the refuge screen.
5. Obtain the six doses by payment, stealth or violence. Donate them and read Neri's changed response. Return to Ivo to see what he knows. His missing-stock response must not accuse an unidentified thief.
6. Recruit Neri. Earn $120 through Mara or cargo, then repair the refuge. Give Neri a Retreat order if you want them at the clinic.
7. Test injury recovery at the refuge. Bandage bleeding first. Confirm no new debt and a limit of 80 health.
8. Compare public treatment with reserved medicine. Treatments occur every 90 active seconds. Reserve policy only binds when the shelf reaches two doses, so a fully stocked clinic takes several minutes to reach that consequence. The journal pauses the world while you inspect it.
9. Save, quit normally and continue. Check the room, fund, policy, patient count and Tally familiarity.

The original jobs, cargo banking, fists/pistol combat, wounds, companion orders and defeat recovery remain. See [Demo 02's guide](BED-AND-BANDAGE.md) for those systems.

## What the review changed

- **Consequences speak:** Neri reacts to paid medicine, illicit medicine, wounded Rook, sale through Mara, rescue, betrayal, an empty clinic and your care policy. Ivo distinguishes paid release, unidentified missing stock, identified offenses, an incapacitated guard and restitution. Quiet optional conversations connect the debt system to their families.
- **Standing is scoped:** the old campaign rank is labeled **MARA / STANDING**. Ivo's grievance remains a separate relationship, rather than pretending a single reputation number describes everyone.
- **Deadline is legible:** the HUD gives a fixed arrival appointment, **21:52**, against the port clock. The exact remaining time stays in the journal and collector screen; a countdown does not dominate normal play.
- **History is honest:** scheduled events use their actual simulation timestamps. Entries show port time with seconds and an order number; simultaneous actions remain simultaneous rather than acquiring fabricated delays.
- **Street quality:** four cars move, their wheels turn, junction access prevents conflicting arrivals, and they yield to pedestrians. Navigation replans around current vehicle footprints. Solid props share physics and navigation; collected props stop blocking routes.
- **Tally:** visible name marker, a pet prompt near the clinic, a short reaching/tail response and a persistent first encounter in the journal.

The Demo 02 dossier informed this slice. The four Demo 03 guided-build reviews and their unchanged scores are collected in [the Demo 03 dossier](Docs/funstra-review-dossier-demo03.html), with a separate CD integration record. ## Boundaries for reviewers

Traffic is local ambient driving, not a drivable vehicle or a police reinforcement system. It has no ramming damage. A person can hold up a lane until they leave. Junction checks are deliberately conservative. Residents and Neri can route around a stopped car.

Edda is a person in Neri's account of the clinic, not a newly simulated patient actor. Public treatment remains a scheduled stock/cash transaction. The refuge adds shared recovery and resource decisions, not property conquest, rent collection, offscreen businesses or a new faction campaign.

No lower-tier physical GPU was available for validation. A 30 FPS cap is a timing check, not evidence of minimum hardware requirements. Rendered captures are staged evidence, not a substitute for a human playing the controls. See [validation](Evidence/VALIDATION.md).
