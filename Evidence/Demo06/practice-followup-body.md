## Player problem
Completed medical and mechanical work records practice, but the player cannot readily inspect medical progress and neither practice counter changes a capability. Demo06's new v2 reviews identify this as missing present-game depth.

## Bounded follow-up
Make earned practice visible at the relevant completed action and in crew inspection. Select one useful, bounded effect for medical or mechanical competence during a connected subsequent task. Preserve real supplies, interrupted-action rules and one award per completed consequential action. Repeating empty actions must not farm progress. Do not introduce a broad skill tree or weaken enemies to demonstrate improvement.

## Observable acceptance
Compare a novice and an experienced person's response to the same meaningful task. Show the earned change, its resource cost and its effect, then save/reload. Interrupted or repeated no-op actions award nothing. Keep partial delivery and unimplemented competence explicit until observed.
