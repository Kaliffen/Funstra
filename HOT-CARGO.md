# Hot Cargo — next playable slice

The creative direction is a small step from taking orders toward choosing your own criminal livelihood. Mara's three favors remain the authored introduction; cargo gives the streets an optional, repeatable extraction loop.

## Player choices

| Pickup | Value | Load | Exposure |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Arcade spares | $60 | 2 | 1.5-second theft; witnesses can report it |
| Garage tools | $110 | 3 | 2.5-second theft; witnesses can report it |
| Bonded cargo | $220 | 5 | 4-second theft; guaranteed alarm |

The starting bag holds 6 load. At 5 or more, movement is 20% slower. The quiet pair pays $170 and fills most of the bag; bonded cargo pays more but guarantees a chase. Quick Hands shortens pickup time. Other campaign perks continue to affect movement and detection.

Return to the mint HOME circle, clear all heat, stand still and hold E for three seconds. Releasing E, moving away, leaving the circle, opening a menu, or becoming wanted interrupts extraction. Banking converts the whole bag to saved cash and restocks all sites. Arrest confiscates unbanked cargo, applies the existing fine and restocks sites.

Press F at home to buy the $180 courier satchel: capacity 9. It lets you combine bonded cargo with either smaller pickup, but never all three. Heavy loads still slow you down. Banking and purchases persist immediately. Loose cargo does not survive reloading; original campaign saves remain compatible.

Cargo is available immediately, alongside Mara's jobs. After the third delivery, KEEP WORKING THE PORT continues free roam. Finished saves can also resume directly from the title screen.

## Review route

1. Run Play.cmd; enter or continue Old Port.
2. TAB shows named lilac pickups, prices, load and the alarm warning. HOME is the mint square.
3. Try the two smaller crates, return home and hold E to bank $170.
4. Try bonded cargo; break sight, hide by a green bin, then bank $220.
5. Press F at home, buy the satchel and attempt a combined northern haul.

## Boundary

This slice adds voluntary risk, extraction and one equipment purchase. No combat, driving or interiors are introduced. Cargo restocks immediately on banking or arrest; there is no simulated supply economy. Cash beyond the satchel is currently a score/resource for later slices. Human feedback on pursuit difficulty and repeated routes remains the acceptance gate.
